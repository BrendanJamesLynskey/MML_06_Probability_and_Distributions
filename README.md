# Probability and Distributions

Deck 06 of the [Mathematics for Machine Learning &mdash; Companion Series](https://github.com/BrendanJamesLynskey/MML_Hub).

**Live presentation:** https://brendanjameslynskey.github.io/MML_06_Probability_and_Distributions/

Sample spaces, sum &amp; product rules, Bayes' theorem, summary statistics,
the Gaussian (with marginalisation and conditioning), conjugacy, the
exponential family, change of variables. Two interactive widgets: a
Bayes'-theorem updater for a coin and a draggable bivariate Gaussian
explorer.

## What's inside

- Sample spaces, events, probability measures
- Discrete vs continuous distributions and the role of densities
- Sum rule, product rule, Bayes' theorem
- Expectations, variance, covariance, independence
- The univariate and multivariate Gaussian
- Marginal and conditional Gaussians &mdash; the closed-form formulae
- Conjugate priors and the Gauss-Gauss model
- The exponential family of distributions
- Change of variables, transformations and re-parameterisation
- Interactive Bayes' theorem demo and bivariate Gaussian explorer

Companion to chapter 6 of:

> Deisenroth, M. P., Faisal, A. A. &amp; Ong, C. S. (2020). *Mathematics for Machine Learning.* Cambridge University Press. Free PDF: [mml-book.github.io](https://mml-book.github.io/).
